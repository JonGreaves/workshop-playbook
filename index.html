After the workshop

Turn outputs into insight assets

Do not let workshop boards become lonely old documents. Convert them into durable product intelligence.

Decision records

Capture what was decided, why, who contributed, what evidence supported the choice, and what remains uncertain.

Insight repository

Store user pains, research clips, assumptions, experiments, behavioural patterns, and evidence in a searchable structure.

Opportunity backlog

Track opportunities as outcome-focused problem areas rather than jumping straight to feature requests.

Strategic narrative

Translate workshop synthesis into a concise story that explains where the business is, what users need, and what should happen next.
